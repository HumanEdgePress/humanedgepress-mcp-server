Parents of toddlers are constantly marketed "educational" apps promising faster phonics, math, and logic. Yet decades of developmental psychology point to the exact opposite phenomenon: the 2D Transfer Deficit.

Why Glass Compresses Early Comprehension

During the first seven years of life, children build mental models through three-dimensional space, tactile weight, and human contingency. When a book has real physical thickness, a child's hands feel how much story has passed and how much remains. When a parent pauses on a page in S is for Science or the Big Pre-K Learning Workbook (Ages 3–5), executive attention slows down enough for working memory and real conversation to take root.
