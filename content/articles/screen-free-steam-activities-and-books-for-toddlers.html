When parents hear "STEM" or "STEAM," it's easy to picture robots and tablets. But for a two-, three-, or four-year-old, authentic scientific and engineering thinking is 100% tactile.

A 5-Book Kitchen-Table STEAM Routine

  • Science (S is for Science): Test five kitchen items in a bowl of water—ask "What is your guess: sink or float?"
  • Technology (T is for Technology): Hunt around your house for wheels, hinges, zippers, and levers.
  • Engineering (E is for Engineering): Fold an index card into an arch bridge between two books and test how many coins it supports.
  • Art (A is for Art): Take peeled crayons outside for tree-bark texture rubbings.
  • Math (M is for Math): Count, halve, and build repeating berry patterns at snack time.
